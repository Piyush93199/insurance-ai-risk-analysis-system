## System Architecture Summary

The system follows a layered architecture:

1. Customer Data Input
2. Streamlit Dashboard
3. Risk Prediction Model
4. Risk Classification Layer
5. Policy Recommendation Engine
6. Insurance Policy Output

### Key Components

#### Streamlit Dashboard

Provides an interactive user interface for customer data entry and result visualization.

#### Risk Prediction Model

Random Forest Classifier used for customer risk prediction.

Performance:

* Accuracy: 90.30%

#### Risk Classification Layer

Customers are categorized into:

* Low Risk
* Medium Risk
* High Risk

#### Recommendation Engine

Maps customer risk levels to appropriate insurance plans.
