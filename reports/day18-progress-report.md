# Day 18 Progress Report

## Date

Day 18

# Objective

The objective of Day 18 was to enhance the Insurance AI Risk Analysis and Policy Recommendation System by introducing analytical dashboard components that provide meaningful business insights and data visualizations.

The focus was on transforming the application from a prediction-based system into a business intelligence dashboard capable of supporting insurance-related decision-making.

---

# Activities Performed

## 1. Dashboard Analytics Development

A dedicated analytics section was added to the Streamlit dashboard.

### Purpose

* Improve dashboard functionality
* Provide analytical insights
* Support business decision-making
* Enhance project presentation quality

---

## 2. Risk Distribution Visualization

A risk distribution chart was implemented to visualize customer distribution across different risk categories.

### Dataset Statistics

| Risk Level  | Customers |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Low Risk    | 446       |
| Medium Risk | 445       |
| High Risk   | 446       |

### Outcome

The chart successfully visualizes the balanced distribution of customer risk categories within the dataset.

---

## 3. Customer Segmentation Analysis

A customer segmentation chart was implemented to analyze customer groups based on smoking behavior.

### Dataset Statistics

| Customer Type | Count |
| ------------- | ----- |
| Non-Smoker    | 1063  |
| Smoker        | 274   |

### Outcome

The visualization clearly highlights the dominance of non-smoker customers and demonstrates the significance of smoking status as a risk factor.

---

## 4. Model Performance Dashboard

A performance monitoring section was added to display key machine learning metrics.

### Metrics Displayed

* Classification Accuracy: 90.30%
* Optimized Random Forest R² Score: 89.38%

### Outcome

Users can now evaluate model effectiveness directly from the dashboard.

---

## 5. Business Insights Section

A business insights panel was implemented to communicate important findings derived from data analysis and machine learning results.

### Insights Added

* Smokers contribute significantly to high-risk classifications.
* Risk categories are evenly distributed.
* Random Forest achieved the best predictive performance.
* Policy recommendation engine supports insurance decision-making.

### Outcome

The dashboard now provides both technical outputs and business-oriented insights.
