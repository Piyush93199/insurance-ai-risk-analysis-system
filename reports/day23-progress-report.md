## Objective

The objective of Day 23 was to improve the transparency and presentation of the recommendation module by introducing workflow visualization and recommendation review components.

The focus was on making the recommendation process easier to understand while improving the overall dashboard presentation.

---

## Work Completed Today

Today's work focused on enhancing the recommendation section of the dashboard without modifying the underlying machine learning model.

Additional dashboard components were implemented to clearly explain how recommendations are generated and to provide a structured review of the recommendation process.
