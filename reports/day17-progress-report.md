# Day 17 Progress Report

## Date

Day 17

---

## Objective

To improve the presentation and usability of prediction results generated by the Insurance AI Risk Analysis and Policy Recommendation System dashboard.

The primary focus was on enhancing visual feedback, improving result interpretation, and providing a more professional user experience.

---

# Activities Performed

## 1. Risk Visualization Enhancement

Implemented a color-coded risk indicator system for displaying prediction results.

### Low Risk

* Green indicator
* Success message styling

### Medium Risk

* Yellow indicator
* Warning message styling

### High Risk

* Red indicator
* Alert message styling

---

## 2. Recommendation Card Development

Designed and implemented a professional recommendation card for displaying insurance policy recommendations.

### Features

* Highlighted policy recommendation
* Improved readability
* Professional card design
* Enhanced visual hierarchy

---

## 3. Customer Analysis Summary

Added a customer summary section displaying submitted information.

### Information Displayed

* Age
* BMI
* Number of Children
* Smoking Status
* Region

---

## 4. Risk Interpretation Module

Implemented an explanation section to provide additional context regarding predicted risk levels.

### Outputs

* Low Risk Explanation
* Medium Risk Explanation
* High Risk Explanation

## 5. Analysis Completion Feedback

Added a success notification after prediction generation.

### Benefits

* Improved user interaction
* Confirmation of successful analysis
* Better dashboard responsiveness

Issue:

Prediction results appeared cluttered after introducing new UI elements.

### Resolution
