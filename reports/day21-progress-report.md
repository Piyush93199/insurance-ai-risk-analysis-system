# Day 21 Progress Report

## Project

Insurance AI Risk Analysis and Policy Recommendation System

## Day

21

---

## Objective

The objective of Day 21 was to improve the recommendation engine by enhancing policy mapping logic, increasing recommendation transparency, and providing additional decision-support information to users.

The focus was on making insurance recommendations more informative and easier to understand.

---

## Work Completed Today

Today's work focused on enhancing the recommendation engine component of the application.

Previously, recommendations consisted only of policy names linked to risk categories. The system was upgraded to provide detailed policy information, recommendation explanations, and insurance plan comparisons.

---

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Recommendation Logic Review

The existing recommendation engine was reviewed to understand how risk classifications were mapped to insurance plans.

#### Existing Mapping

* Low Risk → Basic Insurance Plan
* Medium Risk → Standard Insurance Plan
* High Risk → Premium Insurance Plan

Status: Completed
