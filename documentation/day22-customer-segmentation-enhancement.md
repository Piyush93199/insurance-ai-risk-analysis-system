# Day 22 Customer Segmentation Enhancement

## Objective

The objective of Day 22 was to enhance the customer analysis capabilities of the Insurance AI Risk Analysis and Policy Recommendation System by introducing detailed customer profiling, segmentation analysis, and risk group interpretation.

The focus was on providing users with a clearer understanding of customer characteristics and how these attributes influence insurance risk assessment and policy recommendations.

---

# Overview

Customer segmentation is an essential component of insurance analytics. By organizing customer information into meaningful groups, insurance providers can better understand customer profiles, evaluate potential risks, and recommend appropriate insurance products.

Several new analytical components were added to improve customer-centric decision support.

---

# Enhancements Implemented

## 1. Customer Profile Summary

A customer profile summary section was implemented to display demographic and lifestyle information in a structured table.

### Information Displayed

* Age
* Gender
* Body Mass Index (BMI)
* Number of Children
* Smoking Status
* Region

### Purpose

* Provide a complete customer overview.
* Improve readability of customer information.
* Support insurance profile analysis.

---

## 2. Customer Segmentation Summary

A customer segmentation summary module was introduced to classify customers based on lifestyle indicators.

### Segments

#### General Lifestyle Segment

Assigned to non-smoker customers.

Characteristics:

* Lower health-related risk
* Standard insurance eligibility
* Lower expected claim probability

---

#### High-Risk Lifestyle Segment

Assigned to smoker customers.

Characteristics:

* Higher health-related risk
* Increased claim probability
* Enhanced insurance coverage recommended

---

### Benefits

* Simplifies customer categorization.
* Improves understanding of lifestyle-based risk.
* Supports business decision-making.

---

## 3. Risk Group Analysis

A dedicated risk interpretation module was added to provide descriptive information for each predicted risk category.

### Low Risk Group

* Healthy customer profile
* Lower insurance claim probability
* Eligible for affordable insurance plans

---

### Medium Risk Group

* Moderate insurance exposure
* Balanced premium recommendation
* Periodic policy review suggested

---

### High Risk Group

* Higher insurance claim probability
* Comprehensive insurance coverage recommended
* Premium protection advised

---

### Benefits

* Improves prediction interpretability.
* Explains machine learning outputs.
* Supports customer understanding.

---

## 4. Customer Characteristics Table

A customer characteristics table was implemented to summarize the customer's overall assessment.

### Information Included

| Category         | Assessment                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Age Group        | Adult / Senior                  |
| Lifestyle        | Smoker / Non-Smoker             |
| Insurance Risk   | Predicted Risk Category         |
| Recommended Plan | Insurance Policy Recommendation |

### Benefits

* Consolidates important customer information.
* Simplifies dashboard interpretation.
* Supports insurance decision-making.
